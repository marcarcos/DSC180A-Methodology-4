1. __What is the most interesting topic covered in your domain this quarter?__
  - The most interesting topic covered so far is dynamics. We are learning ordinary differential equations to study the dynamics of a nonlinear metamaterial chain. The chain has springs such that the coefficients of the springs can be tuned to affect how the impact force on the chain distributes the kinetic energy. The goal is to minimize KE ratio from nonlinear coefficients to linear coefficients. What has been most interesting is studying the dynamical system to generate different designs for structure discovery. So, knowing how to expand the design space to generate the richest data to feed into a model. 
2. __Describe a potential investigation you would like to pursue for your Quarter 2 Project.__
  - I have been talking to my mentors and a potential path to take is feature discovery in these dynamical systems. Currently, it's very difficult to associate spring coefficients to known physical phenomena (like bistability). The task is to be able to look at a raw dynamical system and have a model associate the tunable parameters with interpretable features that can be explained by physical phenomena.
